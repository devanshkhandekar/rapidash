# Code of Conduct

We want Rapidash to be a welcoming and productive open-source project.

Contributors and maintainers agree to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Focus feedback on technical decisions and their impact rather than personal criticism.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing others' private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to uphold these expectations.

For conduct concerns, contact maintainers through a private channel made available in the repository profile or relevant project organization. **Before inviting external contributions, maintainers should provide a specific confidential contact route in this file.**
